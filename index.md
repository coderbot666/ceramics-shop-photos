# Product Photos for a Small Ceramics Shop on a Budget

Handmade ceramics sell on texture and glaze. These notes cover how I shoot listings with a phone and a window, and where generated images fit in.

## Real photos for the product
Buyers need to see the actual mug: glaze variation, base, scale in a hand. Those photos are always real, shot near a north-facing window with a white foam board as fill.

## Generated scenes for banners only
For shop banners and seasonal headers I sometimes generate a styled table scene with [seedream5-5.net](https://seedream5-5.net/) — "rustic oak table, linen cloth, morning light, empty center" — and composite a real product photo into the empty space. I never present a generated pot as a product.

## Consistent angles
Front, three-quarter, top-down, base, in-hand. The same five angles for every listing make the shop look organised.

## Color check
Photograph a grey card in the first frame of each session and correct white balance from it. Glaze colors are the most common reason for returns.
